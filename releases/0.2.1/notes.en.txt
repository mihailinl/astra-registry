Astra now checks for updates on its own and tells you once when a new build is
ready. The About panel shows what is waiting, so a notice you dismiss is not a
notice you lose.

The installer is signed, so Windows no longer warns about an unknown publisher.

Updating asks before closing a running Astra instead of stopping it underneath
you, and the installer follows the language you picked.

Autostart now says what went wrong instead of failing quietly.

The companion pack is no longer bundled into the installer, so the download is
smaller.

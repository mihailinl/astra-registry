If the voice you chose refuses to speak (a lapsed key, a provider outage),
Astra no longer goes mute with a red warning on every reply. It answers in the
free local voice when that voice is installed and says so once; when no voice
can speak at all, it still tells you every time.

Plugins can be updated from their cards again. The Update button had never been
drawn; it is there now, together with Update all and Restart all, and each one
reports what actually happened to every plugin. A plugin that cannot be
installed on this computer no longer carries an update badge that no press
could clear.

Signing in is sturdier. A sign-in could half-succeed and leave you signed in
but refused everywhere; a single refusal from a proxy or a captive portal could
lock Astra out until a restart; and one kind of server answer silently deleted
your sign-in. Each of these now recovers or tells you what happened.

When a download fails on the way, the error now names who refused it, the
delivery network or the server, so a support report can be followed up.

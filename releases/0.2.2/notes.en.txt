Astra can now update itself. When a new build is ready you are offered it once,
and the offer waits in About until you take it or dismiss it, so a notice you
closed is not a notice you lost.

One button does the whole thing: it downloads the installer, checks it against
the signature, installs it and brings Astra back.

Where Astra was installed through Steam or a Linux package, that installer owns
updates instead. Astra says so plainly rather than showing a button that would
not work.

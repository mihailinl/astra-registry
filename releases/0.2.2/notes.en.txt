Astra can now update itself. When a new build is ready you are offered it once.
Close the notice and it stays waiting in About — dismissing it silences the
banner, it does not throw the offer away.

One button does the whole thing: it downloads the installer, checks it byte for
byte against the digest published in the signed release manifest, installs it and
brings Astra back.

Where Astra came from Steam, from a Linux package, or as a portable copy, that
route owns updates instead. Astra says so plainly rather than showing a button
that would not work.
